Frequently asked questions by category

What is Off Label Research?

Off Label Research supplies independently tested compounds for qualified laboratory research. Every product is labeled for research use only.

Are your products third-party tested?

Yes. Available batches are independently tested and matched to documentation by product and lot number.

What is a Certificate of Analysis (COA)?

A Certificate of Analysis is a laboratory record that reports the identity, purity, and other available analytical findings for a tested batch.

Where can I find your COAs?

Visit the testing and document archive, then match the product name and lot number on your label to the corresponding record.

What does 99%+ purity mean?

It means the reported analytical purity for the tested sample was greater than 99 percent. Review the associated record for the exact result and method.

Do you manufacture your products?

We work with specialized suppliers and independent laboratories. Our focus is sourcing, documentation, and clear batch traceability.

What are Off Label Research products intended for?

All products are sold for laboratory and research use only. They are not for human consumption.

Can you provide dosing or administration guidance?

No. We do not provide dosing, administration, or personal-use guidance.

Who may purchase research products?

Purchasers must confirm that products will be used only for lawful laboratory or research purposes.

Do you provide medical advice?

No. Information on this site is not medical advice and is not a substitute for care from a licensed professional.

How do I place an order?

Select an available research product, review its listed strength and documentation, then complete checkout.

Can I change or cancel an order?

Contact support as soon as possible. Changes cannot be guaranteed after fulfillment begins.

Why was my order canceled?

An order may be canceled when payment cannot be verified, inventory changes, or the order does not meet our research-use requirements.

Where can I review my order?

Sign in to your account to review available order details and status updates.

Which payment methods are accepted?

Current payment methods are shown securely at checkout.

When will my payment be charged?

Payment is processed when you submit the order, subject to verification by the selected payment provider.

Why did my payment fail?

Confirm your billing details and contact your payment provider if the transaction continues to be declined.

Will I receive an order receipt?

Yes. A receipt is sent to the email address entered during checkout after the order is accepted.

When will my order ship?

Processing estimates are shown in the current shipping policy. Orders placed during high-volume periods may require additional time.

How can I track an order?

Tracking details are provided through order communications and, when available, in your account.

Where do you ship?

Available destinations are shown during checkout and may change based on carrier or regulatory limitations.

What if my package is delayed?

Review the carrier tracking first. If no update appears after the carrier's expected window, contact support with your order number.

Can I update my shipping address?

Contact support immediately. An address cannot be changed after the order has entered fulfillment.

How should an unopened product be stored?

Follow the storage statement printed on the product label and preserve the product in its original packaging.

What should I do if packaging arrives damaged?

Do not use the product. Photograph the outer package, label, and affected item, then contact support with your order number.

How should research material be handled?

Qualified personnel should follow appropriate laboratory controls, protective equipment requirements, and facility procedures.

What is your return policy?

Eligibility and timing are described in the current returns policy. Contact support before sending any item back.

What if I received the wrong item?

Contact support with your order number and clear photos of the shipping label and received item.

What if an item is missing?

Contact support with your order number and a photo of everything received in the package.

How long do refunds take?

Approved refunds are submitted promptly, but posting time depends on your payment provider.

How do I access my account?

Open Account to sign in, review orders, and manage the details available to your profile.

How do I reset my password?

Use the password reset link on the account sign-in page and follow the instructions sent by email.

How do I contact support?

Email info@offlabelresearch.com with your order number when the question concerns an order.

When will support respond?

Support replies as soon as possible during normal business hours. Complex order or carrier issues may require additional review.
